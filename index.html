Calendar-gmail

A personal assistant for a single user, running on a private server. It requests two Google permissions: Calendar (read, edit, create) and Gmail (read only).

Access is limited to one authorised account. Data is stored locally on the operator's server and is not shared with or sold to any third party. Conversation content may be sent to a third-party language model provider to generate responses.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions.
