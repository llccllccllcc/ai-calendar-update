Privacy Policy

Calendar-gmail · last updated 17 October 2026

Data accessed

The assistant requests exactly two OAuth scopes, and no others:

  • .../auth/calendar — create, edit, and delete calendar events.
  • .../auth/gmail.readonly — read mail only; cannot send or modify.

Storage

OAuth tokens and conversation history are stored on the operator's private server, with filesystem permissions limited to the account running the service. Tokens are not written to source control or external stores. Calendar and mail content is read into memory only to answer a request.

Sharing

No Google user data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking on the public pages or in the assistant. Data is transmitted only to Google API endpoints. Conversation content may be sent to a third-party language model provider to generate responses.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, which immediately invalidates all tokens. Google data remains under the user's control in their own Google account.
