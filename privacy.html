Privacy Policy

Calendar · last updated 17 October 2026

This is a personal tool operated by a single user for their own use. It is self-hosted on a private server, authorised only against the operator's own Google account, and not offered to any other person.

Storage

Credentials and conversation history are stored locally on the operator's private server, with filesystem permissions limited to the account running the service. Data is read into memory only to answer a request.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party. There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking on these pages or in the assistant. Data is transmitted only to Google API endpoints.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time from Google account permissions, which immediately invalidates all stored tokens. Google data remains under the user's control in their own Google account.
